♠ Spades
GAME
♠ broken

Teams: you and the player opposite are partners.

Bid how many tricks you'll take (1–13), or Nil to promise none. Your team's bids add up to the contract. Blind Nil (if on) is bid before seeing your cards, only when your team is 100+ behind.

Play: follow the suit led if you can. Spades are trumps: the highest spade wins, otherwise the highest card of the led suit. You can't lead spades until they're broken (someone trumps with one), unless you only have spades.

Scoring: make your contract for 10× the bid, +1 for each extra trick (a bag). Fall short and lose 10× the bid. Every 10 bags costs 100. Nil is +100 / −100 (Blind Nil ±200); a broken Nil's tricks count as bags.

Winning: first team to the target. A team at −200 loses.
