    🎲 YAHTZEE
    GAME

    On your turn roll up to three times. Click dice to hold them between rolls. Then fill exactly one open box. A zero is allowed.

    Upper section: add up that number. Reach 63 in the upper section for a +35 bonus.

    3/4 of a Kind: total of all dice. Full House 25. Small Straight (4 in a row) 30. Large Straight (5 in a row) 40. YAHTZEE (all five the same) 50. Chance: total of all dice.

    Bonus Yahtzees: +100 each once your YAHTZEE box holds 50. Joker rules: a bonus Yahtzee must go in its upper box if that box is open. Otherwise it can go in any lower box at full value (Full House 25, straights 30/40). If every lower box is filled, it goes in an upper box.

    After 13 rounds the highest total wins.
