    UNONO MERCY
    GAME

    Match the color, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything.

    Stacking: on a draw card you may stack any draw card of equal or higher value (+2 → +4 → +6 → +10). If you can't, you take the whole pile and lose your turn.

    Can't play? Draw until you can. Then play that card or pass.

    Skip Everyone: you go again. Discard All: also discard every card of that color. Color Roulette: the next player names a color and flips until it shows, keeping every card.

    7–0: a 7 swaps hands with anyone; a 0 passes every hand to the next player.

    Mercy rule: 25 cards and you're out. Win by emptying your hand, or by being the last one standing.

    UNO! Call it at one card, or anyone can catch you for +2.
